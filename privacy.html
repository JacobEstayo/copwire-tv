Privacy Policy — Copwire TV Uploader

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Who operates this application

The Copwire TV Uploader application ("the App") is operated by Copwire TV. Questions about this policy or the App can be sent to copwiretv@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App is a private publishing tool used solely to upload Copwire TV's own videos to Copwire TV's own YouTube channel. It is not a public-facing service and does not provide functionality to any third party.

YouTube API Services and permissions

The App uses the YouTube API Services and requests a single, limited permission: the youtube.upload scope. This scope is used only to upload video files and their associated metadata (such as title and description) to the Copwire TV YouTube channel, and to set the visibility of those uploads. The App does not read, modify, or delete any other account data.

Data collection

The App collects, stores, and processes no personal data of third parties. It does not access viewer data, subscriber data, comments, analytics, or any information belonging to anyone other than the channel operator.

Access tokens and security

Google OAuth access and refresh tokens for the Copwire TV channel are stored securely on the operator's own systems. Tokens are never sold, shared, rented, or transferred to any third party, and are used only for the upload purpose described above.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google account's security settings, on the "Third-party access" page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately prevents the App from making any further uploads.

Third-party terms

By using the YouTube API Services, the App's use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the handling of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
